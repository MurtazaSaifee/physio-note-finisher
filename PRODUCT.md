# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Physio Note Finisher is for solo physiotherapists and small clinics with one to ten clinicians. They use it on Android phones between or immediately after visits, often one-handed and with limited time to document accurately before patient context fades.

Their core job is to turn a short post-visit recap or manual entry into an editable SOAP note, a patient-friendly next-step summary, and a reliable session record. They also need quick patient lookup, simple appointment coordination, and easy access to prior records.

## Product Purpose

Reduce after-hours documentation without becoming a full electronic medical record system. The product succeeds when a clinician can complete accurate, editable documentation in minutes, before leaving the session context.

AI assists with transcription and drafting but never blocks manual completion or final clinical judgment. Sensitive patient data, notes, transcripts, and recordings require clear, trustworthy handling.

## Brand Personality

Calm, dependable, and clinically focused. The app should feel like a practical work tool that reduces cognitive effort, makes the next useful action obvious, and keeps patient context easy to scan.

It should be quietly confident, not promotional or decorative. Information order, concise language, and predictable interaction patterns earn attention.

## Anti-references

- Decorative healthcare styling that competes with clinical work.
- Full electronic medical record complexity, dense administration screens, and feature-heavy clinic operations software.
- Gradients, glass effects, translucent navigation, prominent shadows, and coloured side-stripe cards.
- Status communicated by colour alone, small touch targets, hidden essential actions, or AI flows that prevent manual note completion.
- Marketing copy, decorative eyebrow labels, and visual novelty that slows a clinician down.

## Design Principles

1. Put unfinished clinical work before browsing and secondary controls.
2. Keep each screen focused on one useful task, with predictable navigation and clear recovery paths.
3. Preserve manual documentation as a first-class path, regardless of connectivity or AI availability.
4. Use progressive disclosure for filters and secondary options, while keeping primary clinical actions immediately understandable.
5. Protect trust through clear language, durable records, and careful presentation of sensitive clinical information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support one-handed Android use with readable typography, visible 44-point touch targets, and 48-point hit targets. Every interactive element needs a stable `testID`, an accessible role, and an understandable label.

Use text as well as colour for statuses and errors. Meaningful headings use heading roles. Loading, search updates, successful creation, and recoverable errors provide polite live feedback. Voice-assisted and fully manual documentation paths must remain equally usable.
